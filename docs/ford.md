---

## 🧪 Technical Round 1: AUTOSAR Configuration, ARXML & Ports

**Highlights:**
- Focus on **ARXML usage**, **ECU extract**, and **Software Component (SWC)** generation
- Questions on **virtual/non-virtual ports**, **RTE mapping**, and **Simulink AUTOSAR Blockset**
- Queries on **Sender-Receiver** vs **Client-Server** communication
- Asked about **variant handling** in models

**Sample Questions:**
- How do you use ECU extract in Simulink AUTOSAR architecture?
- What are virtual vs non-virtual ports? When to use each?
- How do you configure sender/receiver interface using ARXML?
- Describe the steps to generate SWC from ARXML files in MATLAB.
- What is your understanding of Service Ports in Classic AUTOSAR?

---

## 🧪 Technical Round 2: Testing & Code Quality

**Highlights:**
- GTest-based **unit testing** of generated C-code from Simulink models
- **SIL (Software-in-the-Loop)** workflow & debugging
- Handling **MISRA compliance**, **Polyspace**, and **SonarQube**
- CI/CD pipeline basics (Jenkins, GitLab)
- Discussion on **model integration with virtual ECUs**

**Sample Questions:**
- How do you validate a model using GoogleTest framework?
- What tools do you use for static code analysis?
- Explain the SIL validation process using a virtual ECU.
- What kind of test harness do you create for model verification?
- How do you handle real-time communication mismatch in integration?

---

## 💼 HR Round

- General discussion on:
  - Work experience and domain exposure
  - Relocation flexibility and preferred locations
  - Past team coordination and deadline handling
- Some behavioral questions and discussion on long-term goals

## 🏁 Final Remarks

Ford's interview process tests deep knowledge of:
- **Model-Based Development with AUTOSAR**, ARXML configuration
- Experience in **Simulink AUTOSAR Blockset**, **code generation**, and **ECU communication**
- Solid understanding of **testing strategies**, GTest integration, and static analysis tools

A plus point is mentioning any exposure to:
- Ford manufacturing practices
- Collaboration with global Ford teams in the US or Europe
- Experience on Ford-specific ECU programs or toolchains

---

🇯🇵 Though Ford is an American brand, a global outlook and experience in multi-regional vehicle platforms (including APAC and EU) gives a strong edge in interview discussions.
